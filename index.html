Our Story

Beauty crafted by hand,

Nails By Zoha started in a small home studio with one goal — to make salon-quality nails accessible to every woman in Pakistan. Each set is hand-painted, quality-checked and packaged with love before it reaches your door.

  • Reusable & durable — wear them up to 2 weeks, reuse again and again.
